# Finlancer Website

## A financial home for independent work

Finlancer is a mobile financial operating system for freelancers. The website had to make a complex promise feel immediate: income, invoices, tax planning and goals should work together, not live in separate tools.

## The challenge

The initial site was visually ambitious, but its heavier visual treatment and mobile runtime behaviour made it unreliable on phones. The redesign needed to retain energy and personality while becoming easier to navigate, quicker to understand, and stable on mobile browsers.

## The approach

I shaped the site around a simple editorial system: confident type, bright functional colour, clean product cards and occasional real-world photography. The result takes inspiration from expressive African fintech marketing without copying another product's identity.

The product is always shown as a phone-first experience. Across the site, interface moments explain how an invoice affects available money, tax reserve and longer-term goals. This turns feature lists into a connected story about the day-to-day reality of freelance work.

## What changed

- Reworked the visual language around Finlancer's own green, navy, cream and accent palette.
- Created clearer mobile app mockups with useful financial data, tighter spacing and less visual overflow.
- Added purposeful micro-interactions, desktop parallax and motion that respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Used editorial images selectively to give the site warmth without overwhelming the product story.
- Standardised type rhythm, buttons, navigation, card styling and responsive behaviour across every page.
- Added a branded 404 experience and SPA routing fallback, so broken links still lead visitors somewhere useful.

## Building for mobile first

The most important design decision was restraint. Motion enhancements are limited on smaller screens, expensive visual effects are avoided, and essential content does not depend on animation or browser-specific APIs. That keeps the experience polished on desktop without risking the mobile crashes that prompted the work.

## Outcome

The website now presents Finlancer as a distinct, optimistic product for independent professionals: lively enough to feel memorable, structured enough to feel trustworthy, and robust enough to work where the product belongs, on a phone.
